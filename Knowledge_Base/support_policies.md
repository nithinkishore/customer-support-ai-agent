
# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Order Tracking
Customers can check their order status by providing their order ID.

Available statuses include:
- Processing
- Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered
- Cancelled

## 2. Delivery Policy
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on location and product availability.

Customers can contact support if an order is delayed.

## 3. Return Policy
Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and in their original packaging.

Certain products may not be eligible for return.

## 4. Refund Policy
Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

The refund is generally issued to the original payment method.

Customers should contact support if the refund is delayed.

## 5. Cancellation Policy
Customers can request cancellation before the order is shipped.

Orders that have already been shipped may require a return request.

## 6. Customer Support
Customers can contact the support team for:
- Delayed deliveries
- Refund issues
- Return requests
- Damaged products
- Order cancellations

## Important Instruction
The support assistant should not invent policy details.

If the knowledge base does not contain an answer, it should ask the customer to contact human support.
